ABOUT US

Youth Empowerment Through Meaningful Action

Youth School for Social Entrepreneurs (YSSE) is an organization committed to empowering young people and encouraging them to turn challenges into opportunities for positive social change.

Through learning opportunities, skill development, training, mentoring, and community-focused initiatives, YSSE encourages young people to develop their potential and contribute to society.

Visit Official Website

Our Purpose

Empowering young people with compassion and purpose to create meaningful change in their communities.
